ABOUT ME

I am Daniel Carvalho, a Data and Analytics Consultant who translates complex datasets, from unstructured text and satellite pixels to core operational metrics, into clear intelligence people can act on.

Combining the analytical rigor of a PhD in Archaeology and Artificial Intelligence and being in the final stages of a MSc in Data Science with a passion for business problem-solving, I specialize in building end-to-end BI systems, exploratory analytics, and automated pipelines. Whether diagnosing process bottlenecks, tracking vital KPIs, or aligning cross-functional teams around actionable insights, I bring energy, precision, and a relentless focus on delivering measurable operational clarity. I enjoy everything related to machines, AI, and the potential of tech for tackling problems.
